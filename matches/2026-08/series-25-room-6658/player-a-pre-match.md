## Why this is a new experiment

The deck intentionally learns no skill despite carrying a legal ten-card earth skill pool. This turns on `岩壁巨像`, `岩壁魔怪`, and `岩壁修道士`. `翡翠永生`, `翡翠守卫`, `拜利兰森林熊`, and Jade Baron's hero aura try to maintain a small non-decaying shield that protects the wall. The deck uses consumable scrolls rather than learned spells to clear blockers, so it can preserve the no-learned-spell condition.

## Opening priorities

1. A two- or three-cost resource body: `翡翠守卫`, `岩壁巨像`, or `祝祷祭师`.
2. `沙虫之饵` if Giant Sandworm is not already in hand.
3. One real attacker (`屠魔者杀手`, `屠魔者武士`, `灵兽 辛柯`, or `巨型沙虫`).
4. `翡翠永生` only with a credible shield source; do not keep an otherwise inert expensive hand for it.
5. Mulligan hands containing only the skill pool plan, high-cost Bear/Sandworm, or defensive equipment without an early body.

## First three turns

- Turn 1: establish an earth resource body or Jade Guard shield. Never learn a skill.
- Turn 2: place `岩壁巨像` before additional earth companions when possible so later summons receive +1 life; otherwise establish Monk/Monster protection.
- Turn 3: fetch or summon a center-front attacker. If the opponent has already exposed a center lane, prioritize Killer/Warrior pressure over a sixth support unit.

## Resource and positioning discipline

- Reserve center-front for Sandworm or another actual attacker. Put Golem, Monster, Monk, Priest, and shield/resource bodies in side or rear cells.
- Preserve at least one vertical earth source for `森林之矢卷轴`; preserve earth plus wildcard capacity for `巨石阵卷轴`.
- Do not overexert the intended attacker merely to save a low-value support body.
- Do not spend the last shield casually while `翡翠永生` is protecting the board. Jade Baron's aura makes shield 1–2 persistent, so even one point can be strategically structural.
- Never learn a skill unless the no-spell fortress has already collapsed and a specific learned attack immediately creates a better lethal line.

## When to turn aggressive

- Turn on offense when a center-front attacker is vertical and the opponent's center blocker can be removed by one scroll or ordinary trade.
- `巨型沙虫` is the preferred sustained finisher: its four life and hidden-on-damage trigger should make repeated removal awkward.
- `屠魔者杀手` is a tempo finisher after a scroll clears center because Rapid Attack converts the opening immediately.
- Use `秋枫宝钻` on Sandworm/Xinke only when the first attack meaningfully clears or damages and the reset creates a second legal attack.

## Recognizing key exchanges

- Good exchange: one consumable scroll removes the final center blocker while a ready attacker survives to hit the hero.
- Good exchange: shield absorbs spell damage while `岩壁修道士` zeroes the first spell's hit attack and the attacker stays vertical.
- Bad exchange: tapping Sandworm/Warrior to pay for defense, then having no legal hero pressure.
- Bad exchange: committing `翡翠永生` without a repeatable shield source, or losing the final shield before the opponent's large spell chain.
- Pivot signal: if all physical attackers are gone or permanently locked, stop adding passive wall pieces and consider learning `自然回响`/`巨岩崩落` as an emergency spell finish, knowingly disabling the no-spell bonuses.
