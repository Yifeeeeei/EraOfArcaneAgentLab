# WINDLESS-BLOOD-GARDEN-001 — pre-match driving hypothesis

Game commit: `e6908601d0ffd7f538d6eae22d9bd7c18d5c8ecb`

## Mechanism and finish

This is a Royal Conflict dark self-damage/deathrattle deck, not a conventional spell-pressure deck. Hubert generates Blood Feast; Blood Feast and Sanguine Strike turn expendable friendly bodies into dark resources or hero healing. Robert records friendly damage/deaths and converts every three marks into permanent attack, life, or dark load. The primary finish is physical pressure from a grown Robert plus Rose Reaper. Whisper Hunter deathrattles and Blood Explosion supply reach after blockers have been cleared.

Black Pine Wand is the intended engine bridge: its friendly-target discount should make repeated self-hit spells cheaper. Blood Thorn, Whisper Hunter, Nether Pigeon, White Bone Knight, and Vengeful Dead are deliberately chosen so that killing our own unit is an exchange, not a blank loss. Black Pine Coffin can cash deathrattles from hand without consuming board slots.

## Opening priority

Keep an opening with at least one cheap dark source plus one engine/payoff. Priority is: Black Pine Wand; Robert; a 1–2 cost deathrattle body; then Blood Thorn / Elegy Scroll. Keep Rose Reaper only when the rest of the hand already supplies early dark and board presence. Mulligan hands made mostly of 4–6 cost cards, duplicate finishers, or skills without a companion resource chain.

## First three turns

- Turn 1: generate dark with Hubert. Prefer Robert or Wand if affordable; otherwise establish a cheap deathrattle/resource body. Do not spend all dark merely to maximize board count.
- Turn 2: establish a second independent dark source and learn/bind the cheapest self-hit skill. If Robert is present, arrange a self-hit that either grows him immediately or converts a dying body into value.
- Turn 3: aim to have Robert plus one expendable front body and one independent dark source. Clear only the blocker that opens a real physical lane; otherwise continue developing and bank resources for a two-action turn.

## Resource and positioning discipline

Robert or Rose Reaper belongs in the front row only when it can remain vertical to attack; do not use the planned attacker as routine payment. Put expendable deathrattle bodies in the front/center positions that enemy spells must interact with, and keep resource-only bodies off the intended attack lane where possible. Preserve at least one vertical dark source before starting a self-hit sequence, because the deathrattle continuation or Blood Thorn return may require dark after the spell resolves. Do not fill all five companion slots with negative-attack bodies: leave a slot for Reaper or a returned Blood Thorn.

## When to turn aggressive

Switch from setup to attack when one of these is true: Robert has reached at least 1 attack and can survive the exchange; Rose Reaper can enter while an independent payment source remains; or the opponent's central/front blocker can be removed and an attacker remains vertical afterward. Do not treat generic attack spells as hero finishers. The direct closing routes are Whisper Hunter's deathrattle, Vengeful Dead retaliation when the opponent supplies lethal damage, and Blood Explosion where the current legal target/range permits it.

## Recognizing key exchanges

A favorable self-hit produces at least two of: 2 dark/heal from Blood Feast, a useful deathrattle, two Robert marks, a Death Magic Stone/Necklace resource trigger, or Blood Thorn recursion. A bad exchange merely kills a body to gain dark already available elsewhere. Against removal, protect the grown Robert or Reaper; let replaceable deathrattle bodies die. Against defense-heavy opponents, grow permanent attack rather than spending turns on low-power spell combat. If Wand does not reduce Blood Feast/Sanguine Strike as expected, record the exact state and immediately pivot to using those skills only when their resource gain or healing still yields a net-positive exchange.
